# Demo script — 90 seconds

Have ready: the app open on the capture screen, a printed photo of a diseased leaf as backup, phone volume up, and `dist/patta.html` bookmarked in case the network fails.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Show the phone on the viewfinder | "A farmer sees spots on a leaf. The nearest expert is a day away." |
| 0:15 | Tap **తె** | "One tap. The whole app is now Telugu — and so is the answer." |
| 0:25 | Photograph a real leaf | "One photo. The phone shrinks it and checks it before anything is sent." |
| 0:35 | Tap **Diagnose** | "About twenty seconds on a village connection." |
| 0:45 | Point at severity and confidence | "Most apps sound certain. This one shows how sure it is." |
| 0:55 | Tap **Listen**, let it speak five seconds | "For a farmer who reads little, the plan is spoken." |
| 1:05 | Scroll the plan | "Remove the leaves. Change how you water. Neem. Chemicals last — active ingredient only, no brand, no dose." |
| 1:20 | Open **My scans** | "Everything stays on the phone. No account." |
| 1:25 | Close | "Faster advice, fewer wasted sprays, one less crop lost." |

## If something breaks

- **No network** → the sample case appears on its own. Say: "It falls back to a saved case. A demo should not die on stage."
- **No leaf** → tap *Try a tomato sample*.
- **No voice for Telugu on the demo phone** → switch to English for the Listen step and say the phone is missing the voice pack, not the app.

## The question you will be asked

*"How do you know it is right?"*

Say the true thing: it has not been validated against an agronomist-labelled photo set yet, that measurement is step one of the pilot, and until then the confidence floor sends every uncertain case to an agriculture officer instead of to a shop.
